FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

1. When should my daughter visit a gynaecologist for the first time?
A visit is recommended if she experiences irregular periods, severe menstrual pain, delayed puberty, hormonal concerns, or any symptoms affecting her overall well-being.
2. Are irregular periods normal during adolescence?
Some irregularity is common in the early years after menstruation begins. However, persistent irregular cycles or very heavy bleeding should be evaluated by a specialist.
3. Can hormonal changes cause acne and weight fluctuations?
Yes. Hormonal changes during adolescence can contribute to acne, weight changes, and mood fluctuations. A medical assessment can help identify the underlying cause.
4. Will any tests be required during the consultation?
Only if medically necessary. Depending on the symptoms, your doctor may recommend blood tests or an ultrasound for further evaluation.
5. How can healthy lifestyle habits improve adolescent health?
A balanced diet, regular physical activity, adequate sleep, and stress management play an important role in supporting hormonal balance and overall well-being.
